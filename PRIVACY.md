# Watched Blocked privacy policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Watched Blocked is a browser extension that hides YouTube videos you've already watched. This policy explains what it does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

## What Watched Blocked accesses

The extension runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/`. On those pages it looks at the video thumbnails YouTube shows you, and at the red progress bar YouTube draws under videos you've watched, so it can hide or dim them. It does not read, save or send your watch history, account details, comments, searches or anything else from the page.

## What Watched Blocked stores

Your settings (on/off, which pages it runs on, how much counts as watched, hide or dim) are saved in your browser's extension storage. If you have browser sync turned on, your browser syncs these settings between your own devices. Nothing else is stored.

Removing the extension deletes its settings.

## What Watched Blocked does not do

- It makes no network requests of its own. There are no servers, accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking.
- It does not load or run remote code. Everything it runs ships in the extension.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, because it does not collect any.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
